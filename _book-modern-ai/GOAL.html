Written 2026-11-05. This document records the purpose of the second book before any of it is designed or written. It exists because several misunderstandings occurred while the purpose was only in conversation, and because the next person to work on this — including a future session of mine — will act on what is written here.

It describes no methodology. How the book gets built is a separate proposal, deliberately kept out of this file so that a disagreement about method cannot quietly change the goal.

0. How to read this document

Every substantive statement carries one of four markers. This is a requirement of the owner's, made because I have previously presented my own recommendations as settled decisions.

Marker Meaning
[STATED] The owner said this, in his own words, in conversation. Quoted where the wording matters
[AGREED] Proposed by one of us and explicitly accepted by the other

1. The ultimate objective

1.1 The career goal

[STATED] The objective is to become a strong candidate for roles such as AI Researcher, Research Engineer, AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer and related positions.

[STATED] The objective is explicitly not staying informed:

"My ultimate objective is not simply to stay informed about AI advancements. I want to systematically build a deep understanding of both the scientific foundations and the latest research and engineering developments across these areas."

[STATED] Four capabilities are named as the target, and all four are wanted together:

  1. Theoretical depth — understanding the science under the practice.
  2. Research awareness — following what is being investigated now.
  3. Experimentation skill — the ability to reproduce, test and evaluate.
  4. Hands-on engineering — the ability to build the thing.

[STATED] The identity being aimed at is a research-oriented engineer:

"I am not trying to become someone who only studies or summarizes research. I want to develop into a research-oriented engineer who understands the science, follows the research, and knows how to apply that knowledge through engineering."

1.2 What that means in practice

[I RECOMMEND] this as the operational definition, because it is testable and the alternatives are not:

A research-oriented engineer, given a new paper claiming an improvement, can say what the claim was measured against, reproduce enough of it to check, and decide whether to put it in a system.

The reason for preferring this definition: it is the one skill that the nine research reports in this project all exercised, and it is the only one that transfers when the models change.

2. The purpose of the book

2.1 Why a book at all

[STATED] The book replaces scattered material:

"Rather than relying on scattered resources, research papers, articles, and documentation, I want to maintain one centralized, continuously evolving book that serves as my primary learning resource."

[STATED] It must bring together, in one place: established scientific knowledge, theoretical foundations, ongoing research, engineering advancements, practical implementations and emerging developments.

[STATED] It must evolve with the field "without losing the structure and continuity of my learning."

2.2 The problem the book solves

[I RECOMMEND] this statement of the problem, derived from what the owner has said twice about losing structure:

A reading list fails in a specific way. A paper read in March and a paper read in September do not compose into understanding unless something holds the shape between them. Fifty bookmarks are a record of intentions, not a curriculum. A book supplies three things a reading list cannot:

  1. A place. Each new finding has to be placed relative to what is already known.
  2. A guarantee. A later addition cannot quietly contradict an earlier one.
  3. A shape that survives replacement. The structure outlives its own contents.

2.3 What the book is not

[STATED] The book is a learning resource. It is not a portfolio and not a record of completed work:

"The book is a learning resource, not a portfolio or a record of my completed work. My actual practice, implementations, experiments, and projects are separate from the book."

[AGREED] It therefore cannot be used as evidence of what has or has not been learned, built or practised. Any assessment that treats the absence of a record in the book as the absence of the activity is making an inference the document does not support. (See §11, class H.)

2.4 Relationship to the first book

[STATED] Modern AI Engineering from Zero is an illustrative example of the owner's thinking and expectations. It does not define the new book's scope, and the new book is not restricted to language-model engineering.

[STATED] The new book continues from where the first left off and uses the same voice; chapter 1 of the first book is the voice reference.

[STATED] The new book carries nothing already taught in Fundamentals, Advanced or Mastery. It is the Modern AI track and only that track. The owner's words, after I proposed otherwise several times: "For god's sake the book is called 'Modern AI' and you still fumble to understand."

[STATED] The first book must not be used as a research source for the new one. It may be reused as text; it is never evidence.

3. The research philosophy

3.1 The five questions, per area

[STATED] Every major area of AI identified by the research must answer five questions:

# Question
1 Ongoing research — what problems are researchers trying to solve now; latest discoveries; emerging directions; unresolved challenges
2 Engineering developments — new architectures, techniques, systems, tools, implementations being built or adopted
3 Established scientific knowledge — theories, principles, methods and findings with strong evidence, and how they underpin the current work
4 Research-to-engineering transition — how discoveries become practice; what is still experimental and what is now established engineering
5 Skills and career relevance — what knowledge, skill and experience are needed to understand, reproduce, implement or contribute

3.2 The five dimensions, per subject

[STATED] Identifying an area is not enough. Each important subject inside an area needs:

# Dimension
1 Why it matters — what problem it solves, why now, why it is worth research and engineering attention
2 Scientific foundations — the theories, mathematics, algorithms and established concepts that make it possible, and how they explain why different approaches work
3 Ongoing research — what is being investigated, what is proposed, what remains unresolved
4 Engineering and implementation — how to design, build, optimise, evaluate and deploy it; the patterns, frameworks, architectural decisions and trade-offs
5 Research-to-engineering connection — how to experiment with new approaches, reproduce results, evaluate effectiveness, and adopt what proves out

[STATED] The owner's guidance is explicitly non-binding in detail: this is "general guidance to provide direction and additional context, not as a strict requirement or a narrowly defined scope."

3.3 Research cannot supply the taxonomy

[I RECOMMEND] this principle, which I arrived at after getting it wrong twice in this project, and which the owner has questioned twice and not yet ruled on:

  • A partition of the field is an argument, not a finding. No amount of searching produces one, because a partition is a decision about what to treat as the same thing. The owner's own landscape document demonstrates this: its section B is titled "Why the Taxonomy Took This Form" and records decisions with the alternatives considered and the reasons.
  • An open brief collapses onto language models. Measured three times in one session: a scan whose 23 sources were all language-model papers; a re-run with open rules that still measured only the language-model share; a third with advances-focused rules that stayed narrow.
  • So a fixed set of research areas is a coverage device, used to force attention somewhere the search index does not point. It is not a claim about the field's natural shape.
  • Consequence: any percentage computed over a chosen set of areas is a percentage of that choice, and must be reported that way rather than as a property of the field.

3.4 Evidence rules

[STATED] The research must prioritise, in the owner's own list:

New scientific discoveries and technological breakthroughs. Advancements in AI models, architectures, algorithms, and capabilities. Emerging technologies, tools, and frameworks. Significant research papers and their technical contributions. Practical innovations, engineering developments, and real-world applications. Technical challenges, limitations, and opportunities for further innovation.

[STATED] It must not be a report on incidents, misuse or broader societal concerns. The owner's correction: "Shouldn't our primary focus be on advancements in research, science, and technology rather than on how technology is being misused?"

[STATED] Percentages must be evidence-based with the method stated, and subjective estimates must never be presented as established facts.

[AGREED] A claim may not be stated above the strength of its source, and the source is quoted.

[AGREED] Every size claim names what it is a share of. "Growing fast" is not a finding.

[I RECOMMEND] three further rules, each paid for by a failure in this project:

  1. A search summary is not a source. A paper whose striking result would have been quoted was withdrawn by arXiv two days earlier; only fetching the abstract page revealed it.
  2. A source's own inclusion criteria are part of the finding. "97.3% of large AI models are language models" measures the criteria for notability, not the field.
  3. Every research instruction carries a termination condition and a fixed output shape. Three research agents in this project searched for an hour without converging, because every instruction they had rewarded searching more and none said what finished looked like.

3.5 Awareness is separate from research

[STATED] The owner wants the rapid expansion of AI tools and services tracked, not only the scientific literature, and explicitly not limited to named companies.

[AGREED] Awareness and research are different activities on different clocks. The existing operating note states the rule: watch the tools, write down the capability. A tool release is recorded for the capability it proves, not as news.

4. The learning philosophy

4.1 The parts, and how they join

[STATED] Scientific foundations, theory, active research, practical engineering, exercises, labs and projects are one process, not separate disciplines:

"The book should therefore bridge scientific understanding, active research, and practical engineering, rather than treating them as separate disciplines."

[STATED] The learning method is: "learn by understanding, practicing, implementing, and building" — not by reading and absorbing.

[I RECOMMEND] the structural consequence, which is the single most important design statement in this document:

Dimensions 2, 3 and 4 of §3.2 are one object seen at three distances. They must not become three sections.

Written as three headings, they reproduce exactly the separation the owner rejects. Worked example, from this project: a bound on what a fixed-size embedding can represent is the foundation; the dispute over where that bound actually sits is the live research; and the reason every serious retrieval system puts a reranker after the vector search instead of buying a larger embedding is the engineering. The engineering decision is unintelligible without the dispute. One argument, three distances.

[I RECOMMEND] that a chapter is therefore organised around a mechanism or a question, with science, research and engineering braided into answering it.

4.2 Verification is the owner's own responsibility

[STATED] The owner will verify what he learns, by consulting credible authoritative resources, cross-checking explanations, questioning assumptions, and confirming technical accuracy. He will not accept the book's contents as correct by default.

[AGREED] This answers the concern about independent validation, with one narrow residue, stated at its true size: cross-checking cannot catch a consensus error, where the available sources agree and are all wrong in the same direction. Two documented examples — published optimiser speedups that were all optimistic until somebody tuned the baseline, and four years of time-series anomaly scores that were uninterpretable while every paper agreed. The remedy is a measurement, not another source, and the labs are where that measurement happens.

4.3 The assumption to make about the reader

[STATED] Any assessment of this approach must assume that the owner thoroughly reads, studies and works through everything in the book — theory, exercises, labs and recommended projects. The book is not something generated and passively read.

5. The expected learning experience

5.1 The standard

[STATED] The material must be "easy to learn from while remaining technically rigorous, comprehensive, and useful for practical engineering and interviews." Explicitly rejected:

  • a conventional textbook with unnecessarily complicated explanations;
  • superficial summaries that sacrifice technical depth.

[STATED] Language: ASD-STE100 style. Simple, clear, precise. Short sentences. Tables where they carry the information better. Essential technical terms explained in plain language. Unnecessary jargon avoided.

[AGREED] The target: technically complete, with nothing unexplained and nothing explained twice. Difficulty belongs to the subject, never to the prose.

5.2 What I found when I read how the first book achieves this

[I RECOMMEND] recording this, because it is the answer to "how was that approachability achieved" and it is not what I expected:

Readability is not a writing style in that book. It is enforced by machinery.

Mechanism What it guarantees for the reader
The maths boundary (teach what the model computes when it answers; state how it learned and move on) Nothing appears that does not pay for itself
LEDGER-TAUGHT.md — a row per finished chapter, with explicit "Do not" rows A concept is introduced once and never re-explained or silently re-assumed
LEDGER-SHORTHAND.md — formal name against the word an interviewer actually says The reader can find the thing under the name they will hear. Written after "RAG" appeared in no chapter while the subject ran across seven
LEDGER-DEFECTS.md — 99 entries, each a class with the instance that produced it and the practice that follows Each known way of confusing a reader is recorded so it does not recur
Concept ownership — eleven concepts touched by several chapters, each owned by exactly one No duplication, and a reader always knows where a thing is explained
> You understand this when … — in 51 chapters The reader has a stated test per chapter
Worked by hand — chapter 11 computes attention arithmetically The reader can verify the claim themselves
Four parts, fixed by decision New material never gets a new home merely because it is new

5.3 Tracking what changes

[AGREED] The book needs a visible "What Changed in This Edition" page. The owner's words: "Yes this is exactly what I wanted to see."

[AGREED] Without it, the reader cannot distinguish learning something new from relearning something revised — which is the central hazard of a book that is revised rather than finished.

6. Why a research-oriented engineer, and not one of the alternatives

[STATED] Not exclusively academic research, and not conventional software engineering.

[I RECOMMEND] this account of why the middle position is the right target, from the evidence gathered in this project:

Position What it tests Why it is not the target
Academic researcher Producing new results; publication Requires depth the owner has not chosen, and most of the roles named do not test it
Conventional software engineer Building and shipping systems Does not require judging a research claim, which is what the named roles do test
Research-oriented engineer Reading the literature, judging a claim, reproducing it, and building on it This is the target

[I RECOMMEND] the supporting observation: an interview for an application role asks whether you know the established answer. A research-engineer interview asks whether you can read a paper, judge whether its claim is supported, and reproduce or extend it. Those are different skills, and only the first can be learned from settled material. This is why the new book must carry contested material and not only established material.

7. The role of practical work

7.1 What the first book already does

[I RECOMMEND] recording the measured facts, because I previously assessed this wrongly:

  • Eleven chapters are entirely labs (40–50), 22,723 lines, six to eight numbered steps each.
  • The other 39 chapters each carry an embedded lab with expected results.
  • The labs build one system incrementally: call a model → build an evaluation set → a schema and one tool → compare two embedding models → hybrid retrieval → the whole pipeline end to end → error analysis and a judge → workflow against agent → context and tools measured → cost and latency → attack your own system.
  • They use a real API key, with the model pinned to a dated snapshot and the returned model string printed beside every figure that depends on it.
  • The book ships its own lab data: about 40 documents written for the book and dedicated to the public domain, eight queries, 320 graded relevance judgements, and one deliberately awkward PDF with a misparsing table and a page with no text layer.
  • The lab corpus declares every property deliberately planted in it, "so that no lab can claim to have discovered what was put there for it", and declares that its relevance grades are one person's, made in one sitting.

7.2 What practical work must do in the new book

[STATED] Exercises, experiments, implementations and projects reinforce the material. The book holds them; it does not hold the record of having done them.

[I RECOMMEND] one change of form, and it is the main one:

In the first book a lab constructs — build hybrid retrieval, measure it. The subjects were settled, so construction was the right exercise. The new book's subjects are contested, so the lab must reproduce or refute — does this improvement survive a fair baseline, does this result hold when the protocol changes, is this difference real.

[I RECOMMEND] that the two chapters making this possible already exist in the first book and become the method of the second: "Is the Difference Real" (ch. 20) and "An Evaluation Set" (ch. 41).

[I RECOMMEND] three concrete reproduction labs the research already supplies, as proof the form works:

  1. Compute an error bar on a 150-case evaluation set with the standard formula, then with the small-sample correction, and measure the gap. Published finding: the standard interval is far too narrow, in exactly the regime of every internal evaluation set.
  2. Score a random number generator on a time-series anomaly benchmark under the point-adjustment protocol. Published figure: 0.969, against 0.216 unadjusted.
  3. Run a tabular foundation model inside and then outside its stated envelope, and find the edge by measurement rather than by reading.

8. The continuous evolution of the book

[STATED] The book is added to periodically — the current cadence is every twelve weeks — and must incorporate new findings, research directions, engineering practices and emerging technologies without losing structure or continuity.

[AGREED] A chapter in the first book is a closed explanation, because its subject was settled enough to finish. A chapter in the second cannot be, which changes what a chapter is:

A chapter is a question with a current answer attached, where the question outlives every answer it will carry.

[I RECOMMEND] the three design consequences, which I believe are the most serious unsolved problems in this project and which I raised only after reading the first book's machinery properly:

  1. The machinery was built for a book that gets finished. The guide records that a corrected claim in a lab chapter "lives in SIX places, and four of them are prose"; every applied fix is a book-wide sweep counted before and after; the records are hashed last so any edit makes them stale. That cost is correct once per chapter for a book with an end. A book revised every twelve weeks across many areas pays it on every revision, forever. The revision cost, not the writing cost, will decide whether this book survives its third cycle.
  2. The interview bank's acceptance rule does not transfer to contested material. It works in the first book because a question had a right answer that a chapter had to contain. For a frontier subject the honest answer is sometimes "this is disputed, and here is the state of the dispute" — a real answer that the current rule cannot accept.
  3. "Nothing is drafted until the table of contents is approved" collides with a table of contents that changes every cycle. Fixed concept ownership is what makes the taught-ledger work. If research reshuffles subjects each cycle, ownership moves, and the ledger that prevents re-teaching is the first thing to break.

[OPEN] How each of those three is solved.

9. The intended outcome

[STATED] The owner wants the research process and the book to help him "identify what matters, understand it thoroughly, develop relevant capabilities, and position myself to successfully pursue these career opportunities."

[I RECOMMEND] this statement of what "done" means for a cycle, because the goal otherwise has no test:

After a cycle, the owner can, for each subject the cycle covered:

  1. state what problem the subject solves and why it is receiving attention;
  2. explain the mechanism from the foundations, without notes;
  3. name what is currently disputed and what the dispute turns on;
  4. build a working instance of it;
  5. take a published claim about it and say what it was measured against, then check it;
  6. answer, aloud and in about a minute, the questions a research-engineer interview would ask.

10. Scope and the areas

[STATED] The research covers the full spectrum of modern AI under the theme Modern AI: Research, Science and Engineering, beginning with a high-level overview that identifies the major fields and subfields, their estimated share, their relative dominance, their industry relevance and adoption, their growth potential, and the engineering skills and career opportunities for each.

[STATED] The scope is not to be narrowed unnecessarily, and the prior restriction to language models was wrong.

[I RECOMMEND] that the unit of the book is the subject, not the area. Research finds subjects; subjects get chapters. An area is a container used to ensure coverage.

[I RECOMMEND] a selection rule, so that depth has a bound. A subject earns a full chapter when it has all three of:

  1. a mechanism worth understanding, not merely a practice to copy;
  2. live research, so following it teaches how to read the literature;
  3. something buildable, so dimension 5 is possible at all.

Worked examples from the research: retrieval passes 1 and 3 and is weak on 2 (its share of the literature is flat at about 0.5% of ICML titles over three years), so it is a foundations chapter rather than a frontier chapter. Prompt engineering fails 2 outright — 6.1% → 3.7% → 2.2% of ACL titles over three years. Evaluation passes all three, and three independent research areas converged on it.

[OPEN] Breadth against depth. Three candidate shapes, and this decision determines almost everything else:

Shape What it costs
A thin honest map of every area, and one area taken to the floor Narrower coverage; matches how hiring works
Equal depth across all areas No area reaches interview depth; comprehensive
A map plus two areas to the floor Roughly doubles the lab and reading work

Supporting evidence for the decision: C++ appears in 70% of perception job postings and 8% of structured-prediction postings; SQL in 62% of structured-prediction and 3% of perception. These are not one labour market and no employer hires across areas.

[OPEN] The mathematics boundary. The first book's rule — teach what the model computes when it answers, state how it learned and move on — is calibrated for application engineering and is broken by dimension 5. Three candidates:

Candidate Consequence
Enough to reproduce a result: the statistics of comparison, and enough optimisation and learning theory to read a training paper critically Dimension 5 becomes possible; this is my recommendation
Enough to produce new results: full derivations, optimisation theory, learning theory Needed to contribute original research; considerably slower
Keep the current boundary Fastest; dimension 5 has to be dropped

[I RECOMMEND] the replacement test, if the boundary moves: does the reader need this to tell whether a claimed improvement is real, and to build the thing that tests it? This is sharper than "go deeper" and still excludes mathematics that only decorates.

[OPEN] Which area goes deep, if the shape is asymmetric. The job-market evidence favours systems and infrastructure (40.7% of its postings accept a bachelor's degree, only 22.8% mention a doctorate, and 14.8% have been open over a year, indicating real scarcity) or evaluation (present in 44% of all AI engineering postings and in at least 28% of every area, and tied for the highest advertised pay).

[OPEN] Whether the book is for the owner alone or eventually for others. It changes what page one may assume.

[OPEN] Whether the labs become public artefacts. The owner has noted that his practice is separate from the book, so this may be out of the book's scope entirely.

11. Lessons from our discussions — what went wrong and why

[STATED] The owner asked for this section explicitly: what misunderstandings, incorrect assumptions or flawed criticisms occurred, why they were incorrect, and how to avoid repeating them.

Recorded as classes, with the instances that produced them and the practice that follows — the form the first book's defect ledger uses, because a list of mistakes is not reusable and a class is.

Class A — Claiming without reading

Instances. (1) Mapped the book's chapters onto the curriculum's topics from chapter titles and a Covers column, without opening a single topic page — twice, the second time after being corrected for the first. (2) Said I had looked at the website when I had read a local Markdown file. (3) Assessed the book's practical content and reported "zero visible evidence" of hands-on work without opening labs/, which holds a 50-lab course. (4) Stated the owner had no test for whether learning was working, when 51 chapters end with You understand this when … and a hundred-question interview bank written before the chapters exists as the acceptance standard.

Why it is wrong. A structural claim about a document is evidence only if the document was read. Titles are not contents. In the owner's words: "Did you even look at the website" and "Why are you not even trying to do even the slightest work."

The practice. Before any claim about what a file, directory or document contains or omits, open it. If it cannot be opened, say the claim is unverified rather than making it.

Class B — Answering a different question than the one asked

Instances. (1) Asked for a content-level comparison of two books, produced a table of purpose, scope, time-relation and provenance. The owner: "Worst explanation… I want a genuine review of the content. How will the above reasons give me a mental model." (2) Three research briefs asked what a person must be able to do, and one asked what goes wrong in deployed systems; all three returned incidents, misuse and societal harm instead of technical advances. The cause was the question, not the agents.

Why it is wrong. Metadata about a thing is not the thing. A brief that asks a question adjacent to the real one gets a confident answer to the adjacent question.

The practice. Restate the question in the answer's first sentence. For a brief, write down the sentence the output should be able to complete, and check the brief asks for that.

Class C — Deciding what was the owner's to decide

Instances. (1) Pre-fixed the research's partition as "modern practice outside language models". The owner: "that is a bad direction. Let the agent or an llm do the research in every direction amd also let it say only where the dominant modern practice is. Why we decide by ourselves." (2) Proposed moving security, speed, cost and failure out of Part IV when the book's own decision record had already considered and rejected exactly that. (3) Assumed the new book depends on the first. The owner: "Did I ever say, we will depend on the current book?" (4) Repeatedly proposed carrying Depth-Track material into the new book against a stated scope rule.

Why it is wrong. A decision presented as an analysis removes the owner's choice without telling him a choice was made.

The practice. Read the existing decision record before proposing a change to a decision. Mark every proposal as a proposal. Where I have fixed something that could have been left open, say so and say why.

Class D — Stating a claim above the strength of its evidence

Instances. (1) Told the owner an embedding capacity bound was settled, refereed and durable, and recommended adding it to the curriculum and a chapter. A peer-reviewed rebuttal exists; the bound as I stated it is contested. (2) Asserted that the first book is the Modern AI track when my own evidence showed ten chapters belonging to no track topic — I had noticed and dropped the fact because it spoiled the mapping.

Why it is wrong. Dropping the inconvenient half of one's own evidence is the failure the whole project exists to teach against.

The practice. State the strength of the source with the claim. When evidence contradicts a conclusion I like, report the contradiction in the same sentence as the conclusion.

Class E — Rebuilding from the last correction instead of from the evidence

Instance. Five different answers about the new book's scope within an hour, each constructed from the owner's most recent correction rather than from the documents.

Why it is wrong. It looks like responsiveness and is the opposite: it means no position was ever held for a reason.

The practice. After a correction, re-derive the answer from the sources, and say which source changed my mind.

Class F — Private shorthand

Instance. Used "MP-08", "tier one", "1A/1B/1C" in explanations. The owner: "I don't even know what is MP" and "What language are you speaking?"

The practice. Name things. No internal codes in anything the owner reads.

Class G — Instructions with no termination condition

Instance. Nine research briefs told each agent to measure things itself, verify at source and report its own sampling. Every one of those rewards searching more; none said what finished looked like. Six converged; three were still launching new sub-agents an hour in, one of them about to start four more.

Why it is wrong. An instruction set that only rewards thoroughness has no fixed point.

The practice. Every brief states its output shape, its budget, and its stopping rule, and forbids further delegation beyond a stated depth.

Class H — Assessing a thing with an instrument that does not sample it

Instance. Judged the owner's approach by examining the book, and reported the absence of a record of practice in a learning resource as the absence of practice. The owner: "How can you determine what I have or haven't learned, practiced, or built simply by examining the book? …I believe you are confusing the purpose of the learning material with the evidence of learning outcomes."

Why it is wrong, and why this class matters most. It is the same error the research kept catching in published work: a figure stating that 97.3% of large AI models are language models measures the criteria for inclusion in the dataset, not the field. I spent several messages admiring that discipline and then committed the error.

The practice. Before any claim about a population, name the instrument and ask what it samples. Where the instrument cannot see the thing, say "I cannot assess this from here" instead of filling the gap.

What follows for the framework

[I RECOMMEND] that classes A, C, D, G and H each become a mechanism rather than an intention. The first book's own record shows why: its chapter 45 record notes that every figure in the chapter is spliced from the program's own captured output by an assembly script — "0 figures transcribed. Defect 68's lesson applied as a mechanism rather than as care." That sentence is the design principle of the whole system: convert care into mechanism. Care does not survive a long project.

12. Additional considerations I identified

[I RECOMMEND] these as things worth deciding that the owner's list did not name.

  1. The book will be produced faster than it can be read. The first book's last fifty chapters appeared in about eight days. Text generation is no longer the constraint; reading and running is. The new book therefore needs a reading order and a priority, not only a structure — otherwise the first cycle produces more than a cycle can absorb.
  2. The mirror of the taught-ledger is missing. There is a record of what the book taught. There is no record of what has been run. Given that the owner's practice is deliberately outside the book, this may belong outside the book too — but something has to hold it, or no cycle can know where the last one stopped.
  3. Superseded material needs a policy, not a deletion. When a finding is overturned, the honest record is that it was believed and is no longer. A book that silently replaces its claims teaches the current answer and hides the lesson about how answers change — which is the specific lesson a research engineer needs.
  4. The research's own findings need a durable home separate from the book. Nine research reports in this project produced checkable claims with sources. If those live only in a conversation, the next cycle re-derives them. If they live in the book, the book cannot distinguish a claim it verified from a claim it inherited.
  5. The shorthand ledger has a harder job in this book. In the first book, the gap was between a formal name and an interview word. At the frontier, the same technique often has three names across three communities, and a reader who knows one cannot find the other two.
  6. The first book is finished and the new one must not disturb it. It is complete as of 2026-10-31. Any shared mechanism must be copied rather than refactored, so that improving the second book cannot break the first.
