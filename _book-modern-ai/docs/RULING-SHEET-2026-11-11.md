# Ruling sheet — the 63 candidate areas

**Written 2026-11-11 for step 4 part 3, and corrected the same day** (a `Below` column held counts
I had derived by splitting strings rather than reading the records; 43 of the 63 records say
`BELOW: none` and the column did not show that. It is removed, and the per-area notes the records
carry are here instead).

`GOAL.md` §3.3 `[AGREED 2026-11-06]` gives the owner "approves, redraws or rejects the proposed
list, with the evidence for each boundary in front of him". **This sheet is that evidence and
nothing more.** It adds no judgement about whether an area is important, large or worth covering:
that is his, and the consolidation run was forbidden from making it.

**Nothing here is a recommendation.** The grouping is by the rule's own three modes; the ordering
inside each group is by how many sources attest the boundary. Both are facts from the record and
neither is a ranking.

**Source:** `../research/frames/CONSOLIDATED-2026-11-07-g.md` — 63 areas from 283 input
categories. Each area's full record is there under its name: sources, what it covers, what it
excludes, what was merged into it, and every finer category filed below it **by name**.

## How to mark this up

Against each row write **keep**, **cut**, **merge into X**, or **split**. A row left unmarked is
not a decision. **An area cut here is gone from the book**, so a cut is worth a reason written
beside it: the next cycle has to be able to ask whether the boundary still makes sense.

## The shape of the list, as a fact and not a recommendation

`GOAL.md` §3.1 `[STATED]` — the owner's own words — requires every area to answer five questions,
among them **"engineering developments"** and **"established scientific knowledge"**. The 63 do not
divide evenly across the three modes the consolidation rule tags:

| Group | Count |
|---|---|
| All three modes | 7 |
| Science and research only | 21 |
| Engineering only | 23 |
| Research and engineering | 6 |
| Science and engineering | 3 |
| Science only | 2 |
| Research only | 1 |

**24 of the 63 carry no engineering tag and 23 carry no science or research tag; 16 bridge.**
(24 + 23 + 16 = 63.) A mode tag records which of the three modes the *sources* attach to that
boundary — **it does not say the area has no engineering in it**, only that the filing systems read
do not file it that way. An engineering-only area still has to answer question 3, and the sources
here do not supply it.

**What this is not.** §3.2 ends with the owner's own `[STATED]` line that his guidance is *"general
guidance to provide direction and additional context, not as a strict requirement or a narrowly
defined scope."* So this table is a fact about the list, not a test it has to pass, and not a reason
to keep or cut anything.

**1. Six areas rest on scope the source may not have written** — marked ⚠ above: `Graphs`,
`Harness Engineering`, `causal reasoning`, `learning on graphs and other geometries & topologies`,
`Software Factories`, `Design Engineering`. For these six no scope statement was found on the source
page, so the one-clause gloss is the framing run's own words. `DIAGNOSIS-2026-11-07.md` §8 has the
mechanism. **The caveat was recorded as "thirteen of the 63" until 2026-11-11**: thirteen categories
are thin, but seven of those sit below an area rather than among the 63.

**2. Thirty-two labels never got a verdict, and none of them names a subject.** Run `g` put them in
an appendix; the consolidation run judged the 283 units in the main list and never opened the
appendix. Here they are in full, so the judgement is yours and not a summary of mine:

## What the chosen level loses, in the run's own account

The run states four losses, and they are the places a redraw would bite:

1. **A general subject named only by a single-modality venue is filed under that modality.** CVPR's
   `Machine learning (other than deep learning)` and ACL's `Language Models`, `Safety and Alignment
   in LLMs` and `LLM Efficiency` sit below Computer vision and Natural Language Processing. Their
   subjects are not vision or language subjects.
2. **The MLCommons apparatus collapses into `Evals`** — 25 suites, groups and benchmark Areas.
   Storage, power and content-safety measurement end up one line apart, and **`Power` is the only
   energy category in the whole register**.
3. **AAMAS's eleven areas collapse into `Multiagent Systems`**, with about 150 topics below them
   unnamed, because the framing run reported them as counts rather than categories.
4. **Two umbrella records sit above the level and are still listed as areas** — marked † above.
