# What a full reading found — 2026-11-07

**Why this file exists.** The owner asked how I could claim the documents were correct when every
check I had run was a pattern match over strings. The honest answer was that I could not: the last
reader audit predated eight topics of decisions, and about a fifth of the current content had never
been read by anyone. He told me to read everything, note everything, and only then fix.

**What was read.** `GOAL.md` and `GUIDE.md` in full. `PROCESS.md` and `GATES.md` in full across two
passes. `ROADMAP.md`, `PLAN.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `ACCEPTANCE.md`, `RECORD-TEMPLATE.md`, all three
ledgers, both `.tsv` comment blocks, `CLAUDE.md`, all four directory READMEs, and all eight briefs.

**What was not read in this pass**, and is therefore not covered below: the four framing-run outputs
and the merged candidate list in `research/frames/` — research output rather than documents.

**43 findings.** Every one was missed by three rounds of reader audits and by every assertion script
run during the eight topics. **Thirty-one of the forty-three were created by the decisions of
2026-11-06 and 2026-11-07** — each decision landed in the file it was about and failed to reach one
or two others.

## A — Would change what the research returns

**A1. The research brief judges buildability against hardware the owner does not have.**
`briefs/TEMPLATE-area-research.md`, `BUILDABLE` (b): "if that still needs more than one
accelerator". Topic 5 established the machine as an Apple Silicon Mac with no discrete GPU and no
cluster. The same phrase was corrected in `pass-3-lab.md` and left here. This feeds test 3 of the
selection rule, so it affects every subject's verdict.

**A2. The same brief never states the machine at all.** A scout judging what reproducing a result
takes has no baseline. The brief may not read `GUIDE.md`, so the machine description has to be
copied in — which `CLAUDE.md`'s exception permits.

**A3. The research brief asks for cost "if it is material".** `GUIDE.md` §6 requires memory, disk,
effort and cost, and says "All four are required, not conditional."

**A4. `pass-2-claims.md` is told to check the answer key and was never given it.** Topic 4 moved the
answers to `markdown/ZZ-review-answers.md` and added an `<ANSWERS>` placeholder to pass 1 and pass
4 only. Pass 2's check 3 names the answer key; its budget says it "compares two files and nothing
else". `GUIDE.md` §5 asserts three of the four passes check the answers — pass 2 is one of the three
and cannot.

**A5. `pass-2-claims.md` check 4 accepts any stated denominator.** `GUIDE.md` §5 and `CLAUDE.md`
require a denominator *somebody else defined* and forbid a share of "the field" outright. As
written the pass would accept "5% of the field" if a denominator were named.

**A6. `claim-verifier.md` is asked to apply a condition it cannot evaluate.** It must decide whether
an all-`NEITHER` subject is `SHORT-ENTRY` "unless the research's `SKILLS` field says a practitioner
is expected to know it". It is given one claim row, and its `FORBIDDEN` bars it from checking
anything else.

## B — Structural gaps

**B7. The cycle has no framing or consolidation stage.** `PROCESS.md` §5 lists RESEARCH as "one
scout per area". `research/frames/README.md` says two framing runs happen **per cycle**, and
`ROADMAP.md` makes consolidation part of establishing the areas. Either the areas are re-framed
every twelve weeks and the cycle is missing two stages, or they are set once and the README is
wrong. The documents assert both.

> **[2026-11-07] Half closed.** The count is settled — **one framing run, not two**
> (`DIAGNOSIS-2026-11-07.md` §5.3), and `PROCESS.md` §8 and the `frames/README.md` now agree with
> each other and with `ROADMAP.md` step 4. **The "per cycle" question is still open**: framing and
> consolidation run at step 4 and on an approved `RECARVE`, not every twelve weeks, and that is what

**B8. `PROCESS.md` §8's agent table lists six agents.** There are eight briefs and eight roles. The
framer and the consolidator are absent from the table that enumerates every agent and whether it
may delegate.

**B9. `PROCESS.md` §4.6's termination argument no longer holds.** The schema defines `TARGET` as "how
many units. A cap, not a goal", and terminating rule 1 says "the unit count is capped". The framing

no target. What replaces the cap — the framer's two floors, the consolidator's finite input — is

**B10. `briefs/README.md`'s schema section describes two kinds of brief and there are three.** The

`TARGET`.

**B11. `GOAL.md` §12 item 1 requires "a reading order and a priority".** Nothing anywhere provides
one, nothing enforces it, and `GATES.md`'s "What no gate reads" does not list it.

## What this says about the method

**Thirty-one of forty-three were created in two days, by decisions that were correct.** The failure
is not the decisions and not the documents' design. It is that **a decision touches four to seven
files and there is no mechanism that makes it reach all of them.** `PROCESS.md` §11 already
identified cross-file drift as the cost of the split and set the merge of the three specification
files for after step 7. These findings are that cost, measured.

**No assertion script would have found any of them.** Each one is a question about meaning: does
this count match, does this pointer lead where it says, does this file contradict the one it cites.
`LEDGER-DEFECTS.md` entry 9 is the class.
