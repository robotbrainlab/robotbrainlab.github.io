# Independent audit, 2026-10-30 — all written chapters

**Why this exists.** On 2026-10-30 the owner found that chapter 45 had had a measurement
dropped from it, with a scope reason given where the real reason was its page count
(`BOOK-GUIDE.md` §11 point 6). They then asked whether earlier chapters had lost content the
same way, and asked for the check to be done by a fresh reviewer rather than asserted.

**Protocol.** One fresh agent per chapter. It is given only the chapter path and told to find
that chapter's own plan row in `TOC.md` itself. No item list, no non-scope list, no
definitions and no opinion from the author. It splits the Covers cell as it sees fit, rates
each promise **taught / merely mentioned / absent** with a quote, lists gaps, answers whether
anything reads as though material was removed, and says whether a reader of that chapter
alone could answer the interview question it exists to prepare them for. Chapters carrying
worked arithmetic additionally had every number recomputed.

**Known limit of the protocol.** The agents are the same model family as the author, so a
shared blind spot would not be caught; and the sources they check against are also the
author's chapters, so a consistent error below the chapter level would read as consistency.
Removing the non-scope list means some findings are correct deferrals to later chapters
reported as gaps; those are marked.

## Result, all 44 written chapters

**44 of 44 audited. 0 rated THIN. 0 audits found content removed for length.**
Every chapter delivers every item its `TOC.md` Covers cell promised. Every chapter carrying
arithmetic had every number recomputed independently — chapters 8 to 44 — and **no arithmetic
error was found in any of them**; in 20 chapters the agent also ran the programs and confirmed
the printed output matches byte for byte. Chapter 44's intervals and every statistical verdict
in it were rebuilt from the shipped judgements and all hold.

Chapter 45 is excluded: it is unfinished and under its own review, which found new false
teaching.

The two book-wide patterns, which are worth fixing as sweeps rather than chapter by chapter:

1. **Unsourced evidence.** Twelve chapters assert a figure, a study or "practitioners report"
   with nothing a reader could follow or cite. Chapter 37 is the counter-example that shows it
   is a choice rather than a limitation: it names `arXiv:2503.13657` and tabulates which claim
   appears in which version of it.
2. **Corrections that reach the body and not the back matter** — `BOOK-GUIDE.md` §14.2's own
   documented failure class. Two instances fixed below; more remain.

## The two patterns worth fixing as sweeps

**1. Unsourced evidence claims.** The book teaches the reader (chapter 6) to ask how strong
the evidence behind a claim is, and then asserts strength without a followable source in at
least four places: chapter 3's "show a reviewer a hundred confident approvals an hour", with
a Note calling it "one of the sturdier findings" and supplying nothing; chapter 4's "68% ran
at most ten steps", whose own record admits the paper is not in this repository; chapter 5's
"practitioners report"; chapter 6's "much of our received wisdom is exactly that". Also
chapter 12's "depths of a few dozen to a hundred are usual" and chapter 13's "the rest clamp
the temperature to something tiny", neither naming a model or a provider.

**2. Corrections that did not reach the back matter.** `BOOK-GUIDE.md` §14.2's documented
failure class, found again. Chapter 16's body refuses to claim that step-by-step working
improves accuracy ("the mechanism cannot reach it"), while its section title says "Why
Thinking Out Loud Works", its Summary says "works because", and Review Answer 2 asserts it
outright. A reader revising from the answers gets the version the body refused. Chapter 2's
"What *shape* means" is a back-reference whose antecedent was removed in an earlier fix; the
record shows a reviewer found the dangle and the rewrite preserved it.

## Fixes applied from this audit, 2026-10-30

Each was reproduced independently before it was accepted, swept book-wide, rebuilt
(0 overfull / 0 missing glyphs / 0 undefined) and recorded with `chapter.py amend`.

**Chapter 20 — a false statistical claim, and the load-bearing one.** The chapter said
"Repeats never narrow the interval that matters for a comparison, because they add no new
inputs." Reproduced and refuted: averaging repeats within a case reduces that case's noise,
which narrows the paired interval on the difference. On the chapter's own eight-coin set the
mean width goes 0.201 → 0.135 → 0.081 at one, three and ten repeats; on a set with no coins in
it, 0.219 → 0.210 → 0.198 and stops. The sentence is replaced by a program printing both shapes
and the claim that holds: repeats narrow a comparison only to the extent that a case's
judgement is noisy, and they cannot touch the part of the interval that comes from having drawn
forty cases. The Warning, Review Answer 3, the Summary and lab step 10 were already correct.
Chapter 20 is cited for method by 29, 31, 32, 38, 39, 41, 42, 43, 44 and 45, so every lab
inherited the error.

**Chapter 16 — the body refuses a claim the back matter asserted.** The body: the mechanism
"cannot reach" the accuracy claim, "because a model may compress the same computation into one
pass and be right anyway". The section was titled "Why Thinking Out Loud Works", the Summary
said intermediate reasoning "works because", and Review Answer 2 gave the mechanism with no
narrowing. Retitled "Why Thinking Out Loud Is the Only Scratchpad There Is"; the Summary now
carries the separate-and-weaker claim with its rung on chapter 6's ladder; Review Answer 2 now
corrects the bank question's premise, which is the better interview answer. 0 survivors of the
old title book-wide.

**Chapter 32 — a Worth Memorising row saying what its own ledger row forbids.** The row called
the answer-was-present-and-wrong cell "generation's", which `LEDGER-TAUGHT.md` row 110 forbids
and the body contradicts. The row now says what the body says.
