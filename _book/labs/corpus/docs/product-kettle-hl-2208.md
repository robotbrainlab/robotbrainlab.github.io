# Harbour Lane 1.7L kettle (HL-2208)

**Code:** `HL-2208-K` (black), `HL-2208-W` (white), `HL-2208-S` (stainless)

A 1.7 litre jug kettle with a 3000 W element, a concealed element and a removable limescale
filter. Boils 1 litre in about 2 minutes 40 seconds.

- Capacity: 1.7 litres, marked to 0.5 litres minimum
- Power: 3000 W
- Cord: 75 cm, with wrap-under storage
- Weight: 1.1 kg empty
- Base: 360-degree, cordless lift-off

The limescale filter is a consumable part under the Warranty and is covered for ninety days. A
replacement filter is `HL-2208-F` and costs 4.50 pounds.

Dishwasher safe: no. Rinse the inside and wipe the outside.

Two-person delivery: no. Ships as a standard parcel.
