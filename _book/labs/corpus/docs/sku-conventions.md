# How our product codes work

Every item has a code in the form `HL-nnnn-x`.

- `HL` is Harbour Lane and never changes.
- `nnnn` is the product number. The first digit is the department: 1 cookware, 2 small
  appliances, 3 lighting, 4 textiles, 5 furniture, 6 garden.
- `x` is a one-letter variant, usually colour. `K` is black, `W` white, `S` stainless, `N` natural
  oak, `G` grey. An item with only one variant has no letter.

So `HL-2208-K` is a small appliance, product 2208, in black. `HL-5512` is a furniture item with
one variant only.

The code is on the product page, on the dispatch note, and on a label on the item itself. Quote it
when you contact us about a specific item; it is faster than a description and never ambiguous.

Codes are not reused. A discontinued product keeps its number for ever, which is how a warranty
claim on something we no longer sell can still be identified.
