# Where is my refund?

Refunds are issued within 14 days of your return arriving at our warehouse, and your bank may
take a further three working days to show the money. So the longest an ordinary refund should take
is about two and a half weeks from the day the warehouse books your parcel in — which is not the
day you posted it.

Check in this order:

1. **Has the return arrived?** Your tracking will say. If it has not arrived, the 14 days have not
   started.
2. **Has the warehouse booked it in?** Your account shows the return as *Received* once it has.
   There can be two or three days between a carrier marking a parcel delivered and the warehouse
   booking it.
3. **Has the refund been issued?** The order page shows a refund line with a date once it has.
4. **Is it with your bank?** If the refund line has a date more than three working days ago, the
   money has left us. Your bank can trace it with that date and the amount.

If the refund was issued as account credit, it is in your account immediately and will not appear
on a card statement at all. That happens when the card you paid with has been closed.
