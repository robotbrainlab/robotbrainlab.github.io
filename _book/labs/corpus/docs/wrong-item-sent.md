# You sent the wrong item

Contact us with the order number and the item you received, including any code printed on its
label. We pay the return postage, send the right item as soon as the wrong one is scanned by the
carrier, and refund the delivery charge in full.

You do not need to wait for the wrong item to reach us before the right one is sent, provided the
carrier has scanned it. That scan is what we wait for.

If the item you received is worth more than the one you ordered, keep it until we ask. Sometimes
the cheapest outcome for everyone is that you keep it and we invoice nothing.
