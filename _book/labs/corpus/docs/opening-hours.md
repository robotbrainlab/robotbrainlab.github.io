# Opening hours

**The website** takes orders at all times.

**The warehouse** picks and dispatches Monday to Friday, 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. The 1 p.m. cut-off for
same-day dispatch is a warehouse cut-off and not a website one.

**Customer help** answers the telephone Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and email within one
working day.

**The shop on Harbour Lane** opens Tuesday to Saturday, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. It is a separate
business from this website: it cannot accept a return bought online, and it does not share stock.

We close on bank holidays. Over Christmas we publish the dates on the website rather than
answering them one at a time.
