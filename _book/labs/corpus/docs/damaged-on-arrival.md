# My order arrived damaged

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery. That window matters: after it, cosmetic damage is treated as
wear and is not covered by the warranty.

Send us:

- the order number
- a photograph of the damage
- a photograph of the outer packaging, including any crushing or tearing

Do not return a damaged item until we have asked you to. We often settle a damaged-on-arrival
claim without a return, because shipping a broken item back costs more than it tells us.

We will replace the item, or refund it if we cannot replace it. Either way you keep or dispose of
the damaged one as we advise, and you are never charged return postage on a damaged item.

If the item is damaged **and** the packaging is intact, say so. That points at the warehouse or
the manufacturer rather than the carrier, and it changes what we do about it.
