# An item is missing from my parcel

Check the dispatch email first. An order can be sent in more than one parcel, and the email lists
what is in each. A second parcel often arrives a day later.

If the email says everything was in one parcel and something is not there, tell us within 14 days
of delivery. We will ask for:

- the order number
- which item is missing
- whether the outer packaging looked opened or damaged
- a photograph of the parcel and its contents as they arrived

A missing item is replaced where we have stock and refunded where we do not. If the packaging was
damaged we also raise it with the carrier, which does not change what you get.
