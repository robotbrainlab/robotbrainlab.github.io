# Making a complaint

A complaint is different from a problem with an order. If something is wrong with an order, the
article about that problem will be faster. A complaint is for when the handling itself was poor.

**Stage one.** Email help@harbourlane.example with the word *complaint* in the subject. A
supervisor, not the person you dealt with, replies within five working days.

**Stage two.** If the answer does not settle it, reply and ask for it to go to stage two. A
director reviews it and replies within ten working days. That reply is final from us.

**Stage three.** If you are still unhappy, you can take it to the retail ombudsman. We will give
you the reference and the details, and we are bound by the outcome.

We record every complaint and publish the number we received each year, with how many reached
stage two, in the annual statement.
