# Who delivers our parcels

We use three carriers and the choice is the warehouse's, not yours.

- **Standard and named-day parcels** go with Pellworth Logistics.
- **Next-day parcels** go with Carrick Express.
- **Two-person and pallet deliveries** are handled by Dunmore Freight, who telephone to book.

Tracking links in your dispatch email point at the carrier's own site. Their tracking is the
authoritative record of where a parcel is; ours copies theirs.

Each carrier publishes its own service updates during bad weather, and those pages are the first
place to look when a delivery is late across a whole region rather than for one parcel.

If a carrier's driver leaves a card, follow the instructions on the card. They are the carrier's
process and we cannot shortcut it from our end.

Carrier contracts are reviewed annually and the list above can change. The dispatch email always
names the carrier actually used.
