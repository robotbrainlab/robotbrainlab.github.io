# How long delivery takes

Working days, counted from dispatch and not from the order:

| Service | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Standard | 3 to 5 working days |
| Standard, surcharged postcodes | 5 to 7 working days |
| Named day | the day you chose |
| Next day, ordered before 1 p.m. | the next working day |
| Two-person delivery | booked by phone, usually 7 to 10 days |

An order placed at the weekend is dispatched on the next working day, so a standard order placed
on Saturday is often delivered the following Friday.

These are estimates from the carrier and not guarantees, except for named-day and next-day, where
a missed date refunds the difference between what you paid and standard delivery.
