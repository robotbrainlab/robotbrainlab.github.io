# Harbour Plus

Harbour Plus costs 29 pounds a year and includes:

- **Free standard delivery on every order**, with no minimum. Ordinarily standard delivery is free
  only over 40 pounds.
- **Free named-day delivery**, which is otherwise 5.99 pounds.
- **Free return postage on clearance items**, which customers otherwise pay themselves.
- **An extra year of warranty**, making three years rather than two, on anything bought while the
  plan is active. The extra year survives the plan lapsing.
- **Early access** to sales, usually 24 hours.

It does not include next-day delivery, two-person delivery, or the postcode surcharges in the
Delivery Exceptions document. Those are charged as normal.

Harbour Plus free delivery cannot be combined with a matched price. You get whichever is worth
more, which on a small order is usually the delivery.

The plan renews annually and can be cancelled at any time; cancelling stops the next renewal and
does not refund the current year.
