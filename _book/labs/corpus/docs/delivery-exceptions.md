# Delivery exceptions and surcharges

## 1. What this document is for

The Delivery Charges document gives the standard, named-day, next-day and two-person charges.
This document gives the postcodes where something different applies, and the surcharge added on
top.

## 2. Surcharged areas

A surcharge is added to any delivery charge for the following areas:

- Scottish Highlands and the islands (postcodes beginning AB36 to AB38, AB55 to AB56, FK17 to
  FK21, IV, KW, PA20 to PA80, PH15 to PH50): **9.50 pounds**
- Northern Ireland (BT): **6.50 pounds**
- Isle of Man (IM) and the Channel Islands (GY, JE): **11.00 pounds**
- Isles of Scilly (TR21 to TR25): **14.00 pounds**

## 3. Services not available

Named-day and next-day delivery are not available to any postcode in section 2. Standard delivery
only, quoted at five to seven working days rather than three to five.

## 4. Two-person delivery

Two-person delivery is not available to the Isles of Scilly or the Channel Islands at all. For the
other areas in section 2 it is available with the surcharge added to the 19.99 pound charge.

## 5. Why the surcharge exists

The surcharge is what our carriers charge us for those routes. It is passed on without a mark-up.
