# Harbour Lane 2-slice toaster (HL-2209)

**Code:** `HL-2209-K` (black), `HL-2209-W` (white), `HL-2209-S` (stainless)

A two-slice toaster with 34 mm slots, six browning levels and a removable crumb tray.

- Slots: 2, 34 mm wide
- Power: 980 W
- Settings: 6 browning levels, plus reheat, defrost and cancel
- Cord: 80 cm
- Weight: 1.4 kg

The crumb tray pulls out from the side and should be emptied weekly. It is not a consumable and is
covered for the full two years.

Matches the HL-2208 kettle in all three finishes.

Two-person delivery: no. Ships as a standard parcel.
