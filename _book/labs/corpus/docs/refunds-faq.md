# Refunds: common questions

## How long will my refund take?

Most refunds are processed within 30 days of your return reaching us. If it has been longer than
that, contact us with your order number and we will look into it.

## Will I get the delivery charge back?

Only if you return the whole order, or if the item was faulty or we sent the wrong thing. Sending
back one item out of three does not refund the delivery you paid.

## Can the refund go to a different card?

No. The money goes back the way it came. If that card is closed we will offer account credit
instead, or a bank transfer if you would rather.

## I was offered credit. Do I have to take it?

No. Credit is offered because it is faster, but you can ask for a transfer to a bank account.

## Is a goodwill payment a refund?

No. They are separate things and a goodwill payment does not change the refund you are owed.
