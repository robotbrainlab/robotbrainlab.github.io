# Updating payment details

Open **Payment methods** in your account to add or remove a card. A card used by an active
subscription cannot be removed until another card is set as the subscription's payment method.

We store a token from the payment provider rather than the card number, which is why a card
cannot be edited — only removed and added again.

Changing a card does not change where a refund goes. A refund returns to the card that paid, even
if you have since removed it; see the article on closed cards.
