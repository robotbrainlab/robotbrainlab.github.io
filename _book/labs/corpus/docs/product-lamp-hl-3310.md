# Harbour Lane floor lamp (HL-3310)

**Code:** `HL-3310-N` (natural oak), `HL-3310-G` (grey)

A 1.5 metre floor lamp with a three-leg oak base and a fabric shade. Takes one E27 bulb up to
60 W; an LED equivalent is recommended and is not supplied.

- Height: 150 cm
- Shade: 35 cm diameter, cotton, removable
- Base: 45 cm span, solid oak
- Cable: 2 m with an inline foot switch
- Bulb: E27, max 60 W, not included

Assembly: three legs screw into the column by hand. No tools. About five minutes.

Bulbs are a consumable part under the Warranty, but as none is supplied the point does not arise
unless you buy one from us.

Two-person delivery: no, despite the height. Ships in one box 155 cm long.
