# Cancellations Policy

## 1. Before dispatch

An order can be cancelled at no cost at any time before it is dispatched. Cancel in your account
or by contacting us. If payment has been taken, it is refunded under the Refunds Policy.

## 2. After dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched it cannot be cancelled. You may refuse the parcel at the door,
or accept it and return it under the Returns Policy. Refusing a parcel is slower than returning
it, because the carrier holds it before sending it back.

## 3. Part-cancellation

A single item can be cancelled from a multi-item order before dispatch. Doing so may change the
delivery charge: an order that falls below 40 pounds after a cancellation becomes liable for the
3.99 pound standard charge.

## 4. Subscription orders

A subscription order can be cancelled up to 24 hours before its dispatch date. Cancelling inside
24 hours cancels the following order instead.

## 5. Cancellations by us

We may cancel an order if an item turns out to be unavailable, if the delivery address cannot be
served, or if payment fails. You are told which, and refunded in full.
