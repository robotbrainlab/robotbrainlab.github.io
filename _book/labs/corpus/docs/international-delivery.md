# Delivery outside the United Kingdom

We deliver to the Republic of Ireland and to most of the EU. We do not deliver outside Europe.

Charges are quoted at checkout once the address is entered and start at 12.50 pounds. Two-person
delivery is not available outside the United Kingdom at all.

**Duty and import VAT are the customer's responsibility** and are not included in anything we
quote. The carrier collects them before delivery. We cannot tell you what they will be, because
they depend on the goods and the destination.

Returns from outside the United Kingdom are at the customer's cost, including on standard items.
The 30-day window still applies and is counted from delivery.
