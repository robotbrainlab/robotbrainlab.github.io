# Returns Policy: clearance items

## 1. The return window

**You may return a clearance item within 14 days of delivery** for any reason, provided it is
unused and in its original packaging. The 14 days run from the date the parcel was delivered, as
recorded by the courier.

## 2. What counts as unused

Unused means the item has not been operated, installed, worn or assembled. Opening a box to look
at something does not make it used. Removing a protective film or a hygiene seal does.

## 3. Starting a return

Request a return in your account or by contacting us. We will issue a return reference beginning
`RET-` and a prepaid postage label. Do not send anything back without a reference, because the
warehouse cannot match it to an order.

## 4. Postage

**Return postage on a clearance item is paid by the customer.** No label is issued. Use any
tracked service and keep the receipt; we cannot refund an item we have not received.

## 5. Condition on arrival

The warehouse inspects returns. An item that arrives used, damaged by the customer, or missing
parts may be refused, and will be sent back to you at your cost.

## 6. Exclusions

Perishable goods, personalised goods and items marked as final sale cannot be returned under this
policy.
