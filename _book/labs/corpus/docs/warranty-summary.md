# Warranty: the short version

Every item sold by Harbour Lane carries a two-year warranty against manufacturing defects,
starting on the date of delivery. That is longer than the one year most manufacturers give, and
the extra year is ours rather than theirs.

A manufacturing defect is a fault that was present when the item was made. Wear from ordinary use
is not a defect, and neither is damage from misuse, from an accident, or from a repair somebody
else attempted.

To claim, contact us with the order number and a description of the fault. Photographs help. We
will repair the item, replace it, or refund it, and the choice between those three is ours and not
yours — though in practice we replace small items and repair large ones.

A warranty claim does not need the original packaging. A return under the Returns Policy does.

The full Warranty document covers what happens when an item has been discontinued, how a repaired
item's warranty is counted, and which parts are treated as consumable and are not covered.
