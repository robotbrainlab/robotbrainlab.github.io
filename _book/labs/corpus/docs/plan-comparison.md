# Harbour Plus against paying as you go

Whether the plan is worth 29 pounds a year depends on two things: how often you order, and
whether your orders clear 40 pounds.

| | Without the plan | With Harbour Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Standard delivery, order under 40 pounds | 3.99 | free |
| Standard delivery, order 40 pounds or more | free | free |
| Named day | 5.99 | free |
| Next day | 7.99 | 7.99 |
| Clearance return postage | you pay | free |
| Warranty | 2 years | 3 years |

Roughly: the plan pays for itself at about eight small orders a year, or at four if you use
named-day delivery. If your orders are usually over 40 pounds and you are not in a hurry, it will
not.

The warranty year is the part people forget to count. On a single large purchase it can be worth
more than all the delivery savings together.
