# Harbour Lane dining chair (HL-5512)

**Code:** `HL-5512`

A solid oak dining chair with a webbed seat. Sold singly; most customers buy four or six.

- Seat height: 46 cm
- Overall height: 82 cm
- Width: 44 cm at the seat
- Maximum load: 120 kg
- Weight: 5.2 kg

One variant only, so the code has no letter. The oak is finished with a clear oil, which can be
refreshed with any furniture oil.

Assembly: none. Ships assembled.

**Two-person delivery: yes for orders of four or more.** A single chair or a pair ships as a
standard parcel; four or more is a pallet and carries the 19.99 pound two-person charge.
