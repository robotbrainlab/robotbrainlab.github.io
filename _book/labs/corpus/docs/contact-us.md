# How to reach us

- **Email:** help@harbourlane.example — answered within one working day
- **Telephone:** 0300 000 0000, Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
- **Post:** Harbour Lane Retail Ltd, Unit 7, Vale Industrial Park, Harbour Lane, HL1 2AB

Have the order number ready. It is in the subject line of every email we send about an order and
begins with `HL-ORD-`.

For a warranty claim, email rather than telephone: it lets you attach photographs, which usually
saves a round of questions.

We do not have a live chat and we are not on social media. Anyone contacting you claiming to be
Harbour Lane on a social platform is not.
