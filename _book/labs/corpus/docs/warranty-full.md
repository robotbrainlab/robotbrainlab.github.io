# Warranty

## 1. What is covered

Every item sold by Harbour Lane carries a two-year warranty against manufacturing defects,
beginning on the date of delivery recorded by the courier. Where a manufacturer offers a longer
warranty, the longer one applies and is honoured by the manufacturer directly; we will tell you
which items those are and give you the manufacturer's reference.

A manufacturing defect is a fault that was present when the item was made, whether or not it was
visible then. A seam that gives way under ordinary use after four months is a defect. A seam that
gives way after four years is wear.

## 2. What is not covered

- **Ordinary wear.** Surfaces scuff, hinges loosen, fabric fades and batteries hold less charge.
  None of that is a defect.
- **Misuse.** Using an item for something it was not sold for, exceeding a stated load or
  temperature, or ignoring an instruction in the manual.
- **Accidents.** Drops, spills, impacts and anything an insurer would call accidental damage.
- **Third-party repair.** An item somebody else has opened or repaired is no longer covered,
  whether or not the repair caused the fault.
- **Consumables.** See section 7.
- **Cosmetic marks present on delivery** that you did not report within 48 hours. Report them and
  they are covered under the damaged-on-arrival process instead.

## 3. How to claim

Contact us with the order number and a description of the fault. Photographs or a short video of
the fault happening will usually save a round of questions. We aim to answer a warranty claim
within three working days.

You do not need the original packaging for a warranty claim. You do need it for a return under
the Returns Policy, which is a different thing and has a different window.

## 4. What we will do

We will repair the item, replace it, or refund it. **The choice between those three is ours.**
In practice we replace items under 50 pounds, because inspecting and shipping one twice costs
more than the item; we repair items over 50 pounds where a repair is possible; and we refund
where neither is.

A refund under this section is the price you paid, not the current price, and is returned under
the Refunds Policy.

## 5. Discontinued items

Where an item has been discontinued and cannot be repaired, we offer the nearest current
equivalent or a refund. If the equivalent costs more, you do not pay the difference. If it costs
less, we refund the difference as account credit.

Where a discontinued item can be repaired but a part is unavailable, we will say so rather than
leave a claim open, and treat it as unrepairable.

## 6. The warranty on a repair or a replacement

**A replacement carries the remainder of the original warranty, not a new two years.** An item
replaced after eighteen months has six months left.

**A repair carries ninety days on the repair itself**, running alongside whatever is left of the
original warranty. So a repair at twenty-three months is covered for ninety days from the repair
even though the original warranty expires a month later — but only for that repair, and not for
the rest of the item.

## 7. Consumable parts

The following are treated as consumable and are covered for ninety days rather than two years:

- Batteries, including rechargeable ones
- Filters of any kind
- Bulbs, including LED units sold as replaceable
- Belts, blades, brushes and pads
- Seals and gaskets

A consumable that fails inside ninety days is replaced. One that fails later is sold to you at
cost, which is usually a fraction of the retail price for the same part.

## 8. Items used commercially

This warranty assumes domestic use. An item used in a business, a rental property or an
institution is covered for ninety days, and several categories are not covered at all. Tell us
before you buy if the use is commercial; a different range is more likely to suit you, and the
warranty on it will be longer rather than shorter.

## 9. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this document reduces your rights in law. Where the law gives you more than this
warranty does, the law applies. This warranty is in addition to those rights and not instead of
them.

## 10. Transfer

The warranty is attached to the item and not to the buyer, so it survives a gift or a private
sale. The claimant needs the original order number, which means the original buyer has to pass it
on. We cannot look an order up from the item alone.
