# Interview-market research

One study, kept here because `_sources/` is the only copy of this repository's research and
because the book cites a small part of it.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `ai-engineer-interview-market-2026-10.md` | 275 coded job postings (208 India-relevant), 67 first-hand interview accounts (54 dated 2025–2026), ~20 hiring-side sources. Read October 2026 |

## How the book uses it, and the line that decides

`_book/` cites the **interview accounts** and not the **posting percentages**, and the reason is
the book's own chapter 6: a set of accounts of what people report is a *practitioner report*,
which that chapter says "supports what people *do* far better than whether it works". That is the
right strength for *what was asked in a room*.

The posting percentages are **dated detail** in the same chapter's sense — a fact with a date on
it, wrong when somebody ships or changes a price — and the study's own limitations say the
`required` shares are inflated, because 185 of its 275 rows are aggregator copies where an
unlabelled list was coded as required. A book that declines to call eight queries a verdict cannot
print *required in 57%* from a non-random sample with an acknowledged upward bias. So no percentage
from this study appears in the book.

Four chapters were amended from it on 2026-10-31 — 12, 31, 55 and 57 — and each records this file
in its own `amended:` line.

**The dated half belongs on the website rather than in the book**, where a 2026 snapshot can carry
its date and expire honestly.
