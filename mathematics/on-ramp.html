Mathematics On-Ramp

The fastest path to a working mathematical foundation. Five core topics, each with one practical course recommendation. Discrete mathematics is covered under CS & Engineering — the focus here is the math that calculus, linear algebra, probability, and statistics are built on, in roughly the order you should pick them up.

Category Topic Course
Foundations School Mathematics Khan Academy — High School Math Algebra, functions, trigonometry, pre-calculus — the foundation to revisit before anything else.
Calculus & Analysis Single Variable Calculus (18.01) — MIT OCW Single-variable calculus from MIT OpenCourseWare. Pair with 18.02 (Multivariable) when you are ready for the multivariable side.
Linear Algebra Linear Algebra (18.06) — Gilbert Strang, MIT OCW Strang’s classic course — the canonical free option. Watch alongside 3Blue1Brown’s Essence of Linear Algebra for the geometric intuition.
Probability Harvard Stat 110 — Joe Blitzstein A full undergraduate probability course on YouTube — random variables, distributions, conditioning, and inference, taught with a story-problem instinct that makes it stick.
Statistics Khan Academy — AP Statistics A practical, exam-style introduction to estimation, hypothesis testing, regression, and the habits of skepticism that statistical reasoning demands.
