Tracking the Frontier

State of the Art

Two views of where Data & Intelligence engineering actually is right now — one slow, one fast. The LLM Engineering Landscape is the conceptual framework: the eight disciplines and seven-project plan that explain what the work is. The AI Ecosystem Map is the live tool layer on top of that framework — which vendors, which products, what’s rising, what’s overhyped. Read the Landscape once and refresh occasionally; check the Map weekly.

The Framework
LLM Engineering Landscape

A four-part essay on the LLM engineering field: the engineering landscape (eight disciplines), what developers actually build (problem-organised), what people actually ship (products + maturity), and the plan (13 study domains, 7 sequential projects, dependency chain, 7–8 month timeline). The slow-moving structural map.

Type Long-form essay Cadence Refreshed quarterly Read time 45–60 min
The Live Tool Layer
AI Ecosystem Map

An opinionated map of the current AI engineering ecosystem — ~150 vendors across 22 layers, organised into six meta-categories (Models, Infra & Delivery, Knowledge & Retrieval, Application & Agents, Operations & Customization, Signal & Research). Each tool tagged for deep-investment / surface awareness / overhyped / rising, with cross-cutting opinions on what’s worth your time. The fast-moving snapshot.

Type Interactive map Cadence Refreshed weekly Filter by Tag, layer, search
How to use them together: the Landscape tells you what category a tool belongs to and why the category exists; the Ecosystem Map tells you which specific tools in that category are worth picking up right now. A reader who only reads the Map sees tools without context; a reader who only reads the Landscape sees categories without product names. The pair covers both halves.
