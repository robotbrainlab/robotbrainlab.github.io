1. What Changed#Link to this section

Two changes, and nothing else.

  1. The Depth Track has three levels. Fundamentals → Advanced → Mastery. Research is no longer a level of the depth path. The old ROADMAP.md §6 was removed and §7–§16 renumbered to §6–§15.
  2. The parallel track is renamed from Modern AI Engineering to Modern AI: Research, Science and Engineering, and it now carries the research dimension as well as current practice.

⬆ Back to Contents

2. Why#Link to this section

The depth path teaches knowledge whose science is established. A settled subject has no open questions of its own, so a research level at the end of that path had nothing of its own to study. The evidence for this is in the roadmap's own content: all eight open problems in ROADMAP.md §9 (Specification) — interpretability as assurance, scalable oversight, scaling theory, continual learning, world models, discrete diffusion, long-horizon agent evaluation, theorem proving — belong to the modern layer. None of them is open in classical machine learning, deep learning or language-model mechanics.

So Research sat at the top of the established path while studying the frontier of the other one. Moving it into the parallel track puts the open questions where the open questions are, and the rename says so on the page.

5. Consequences for the Maintenance Machinery#Link to this section

The research process had to be extended, not weakened, because the track is now named for research.

  1. A new status. The register's lifecycle gains Open problem: a question the field has not settled, carried as a frontier and never as practice. It is teachable, so that the track can present an open question as open. Watching and Discovered remain untaught.
  2. A new scan direction. MAINTENANCE.md §4 now asks the 4-week scan to look for active research frontiers — what researchers are trying to solve, which directions have substantial evidence, which are contested. Before this change the eleven search directions were all engineering-facing, and the word "frontier" appeared nowhere in the maintenance procedure, although the landscape report has a Research Frontier Map. That was a gap.
  3. A new scan action. Open problem joins Ignore, Watch, Current Practice, Investigate, Superseded and Evidence update.
  4. No check was removed. The site build still enforces that a topic teaches only entries the register lists, with the status the register gives them, citing evidence that exists in the report it links to. The phrase check that kept the track from being called a fifth depth level now also catches "fourth".
