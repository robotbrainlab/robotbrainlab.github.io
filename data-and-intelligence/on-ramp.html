Data & Intelligence On-Ramp

Three books, read in order, from no programming at all to building real systems on language models. Each one answers a single question and stands on its own. They assume no prior knowledge of their subject — only that you can read the one before. Read them before the guide, not instead of it.

Step Topic Book
1 The language Python
Python from Zero Coming soon I want a computer to do something for me, and I can’t yet tell it what to do. How do I get real command of Python — not just code that runs, but code I understand and other people can depend on?
2 The science Machine Learning and Deep Learning
Machine Learning and Deep Learning Coming soon I can run a model, but I can’t explain why training it works, and the mathematics is a wall. How does a machine learn from data, and why do the methods that work, work?
3 The engineering Modern AI engineering
Modern AI Engineering from Zero I can call a model’s API, but I don’t know what it is doing inside, what it costs, or why it fails. How do I build something real on top of a language model, and know why every piece of it is there? Last updated — 31 October 2026
