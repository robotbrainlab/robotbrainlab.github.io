# About Harbour Lane

Harbour Lane began in 2011 as a single shop selling kitchenware on a street of the same name. The
shop is still there. The warehouse that serves this website is eleven miles away on an industrial
estate, and the two have different stock.

We employ forty-one people. Most of them are in the warehouse. Nobody works on commission.

We buy from about ninety suppliers, most of them in Britain and Portugal, and we visit each of
them at least once every two years. Our buyers are not allowed to accept hospitality from a
supplier, which is unusual in the trade and occasionally awkward.

We are a limited company registered in England, number 07744210. Our registered office is the
address on the Contact page.

We publish an annual statement on where our products are made and under what conditions. It is on
the website under the same heading and is not summarised here.
