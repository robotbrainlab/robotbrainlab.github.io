# Refunds Policy

## 1. Scope

This policy covers money returned to a customer after a completed order. It does not cover
cancellations before dispatch, which are in the Cancellations Policy.

## 2. When a refund is due

A refund is due when goods are returned under the Returns Policy, when an order is cancelled
after payment but before dispatch, or when Harbour Lane has sent the wrong item.

## 3. How long a refund takes

**Refunds are issued within 14 days of the returned goods arriving at our warehouse.** The 14
days are counted from the date the warehouse books the parcel in, not from the date you posted
it. Your bank may take a further three working days to show the money.

## 4. Where the money goes

A refund is returned to the card or account used to pay. We cannot refund to a different card.
If that card has expired or been closed, see section 5.

## 5. Closed or expired cards

Where the original card is no longer active, we issue the refund as account credit. Credit does
not expire and can be spent on any order. A customer who would rather have the money can ask for
a bank transfer, which needs an account number and takes up to 10 working days.

## 6. Delivery charges

The delivery charge is refunded only where the whole order is returned, or where the item was
faulty or wrongly sent. Returning one item from an order of several does not refund the delivery
charge.

## 7. Goodwill payments

A goodwill payment is not a refund and is not covered by this policy. Goodwill is at the
discretion of a supervisor and is capped at 20 pounds per order.
