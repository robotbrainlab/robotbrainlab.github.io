# Returning a gift

A gift can be returned by whoever received it, without the buyer being told what was refunded.

The recipient needs the order number, which is on the gift receipt in the parcel. If there is no
gift receipt, the buyer has to start the return.

A gift refund is issued as account credit to the recipient, not to the buyer's card, unless the
buyer asks for it to go back to them. That is deliberate: refunding a gift to the buyer's card
tells them it was returned.

The return window is the same as any other item, counted from the delivery date and not from the
date the gift was given.
