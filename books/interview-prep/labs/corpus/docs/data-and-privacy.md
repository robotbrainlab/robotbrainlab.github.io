# What we do with your data

We keep what an order needs: name, delivery address, email, telephone if you give it, and what
you bought. We keep a token from our payment provider rather than your card number.

**We keep order records for seven years**, because tax law requires it. We keep marketing consent
until you withdraw it. We keep an abandoned basket for thirty days and then delete it.

We do not sell data and we do not share it except with the carrier delivering your parcel, the
payment provider taking the money, and our accountants at audit.

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, or for it to be deleted. Deletion cannot remove the order
records we are required to keep; everything else goes. Email privacy@harbourlane.example and we
reply within thirty days, usually sooner.

Our website sets one cookie to keep your basket and one to remember whether you dismissed the
cookie notice. Neither tracks you elsewhere.
