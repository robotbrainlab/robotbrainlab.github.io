\noindent\textbf{Carrier surcharge authorisation --- 2026 schedule}

\bigskip
The surcharges set out on the preceding page were agreed with Pellworth
Logistics on 14 November 2025 and take effect on 1 January 2026. They are
passed to the customer without a mark-up, as required by our published
Delivery Exceptions document.

\medskip
Two changes from the 2025 schedule are recorded here because they are not
visible on the table itself:

\medskip
\noindent 1. The Northern Ireland surcharge rose from 5.50 to 6.50 pounds.

\noindent 2. The Isles of Scilly were moved from Pellworth to Dunmore Freight,
and the surcharge of 14.00 pounds is Dunmore's rather than Pellworth's.

\bigskip
Named-day and next-day services remain unavailable to every area in the table.
This was true in 2025 and is restated because it is asked often.
