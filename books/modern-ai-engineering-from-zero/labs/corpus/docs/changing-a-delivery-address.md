# Changing a delivery address

An address can be changed while an order is **Placed** or **Picking**. Open the order and choose
**Change address**. The delivery charge is recalculated, which can go either way: moving to a
surcharged postcode adds the surcharge, and moving out of one removes it.

Once an order is **Dispatched** we cannot change the address, because the label is printed and the
parcel is with the carrier. Some carriers let you redirect a parcel yourself from their tracking
page, and that is worth trying; we cannot do it for you.

Changing the address on your account does not change the address on an order already placed.
