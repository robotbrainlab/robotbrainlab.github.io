# Delivery charges

## 1. Standard delivery

Standard delivery is 3.99 pounds on orders under 40 pounds and free on orders of 40 pounds or
more. Standard delivery is quoted at three to five working days.

## 2. Named-day delivery

Named-day delivery is 5.99 pounds regardless of order value. Choose the day at checkout. Named
day is not available for every postcode; see the Delivery Exceptions document for which.

## 3. Next-day delivery

Next-day delivery is 7.99 pounds and must be ordered before 1 p.m. for dispatch the same day.

## 4. Large and heavy items

Items marked as two-person delivery carry a flat 19.99 pounds charge, which is not waived by
order value. The product page says when an item is two-person.

## 5. Surcharges

**Some postcodes carry a surcharge on top of the charges above.** The surcharge is not shown in
this document; it depends on the postcode and is listed in the Delivery Exceptions document.
Checkout applies it automatically once an address is entered.

## 6. Returns

Nothing in this document applies to return postage, which is covered by the Returns Policy.
