# Weekends and bank holidays

We dispatch Monday to Friday. Nothing leaves the warehouse at a weekend or on a bank holiday,
and nothing is picked either.

Carriers do deliver on Saturdays, so a parcel dispatched on Friday can arrive on Saturday. Sunday
deliveries happen in some areas and cannot be requested.

Named-day delivery can be set to a Saturday. It cannot be set to a Sunday or a bank holiday.

Around Christmas we publish a last-order date for each service. Orders after it are dispatched in
January, and we say so at checkout rather than letting you find out.
