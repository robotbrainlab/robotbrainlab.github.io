# Price matching

## 1. What we match

We match the price of an identical item — same manufacturer, same model number, same colour —
sold by a retailer on our published list of matched retailers. The item must be in stock at the
other retailer at the time of the claim.

## 2. What we do not match

We do not match marketplace sellers, auction listings, clearance or ex-display prices, prices
that require a membership, prices in another currency, or prices from a retailer not on the list.

## 3. Before you buy

Show us the listing and we will apply the lower price at checkout. Claims must be made before the
order is placed.

## 4. After you buy

If you find a lower price within 14 days of your order being delivered, we refund the difference
as account credit rather than to your card.

## 5. Interaction with other offers

A matched price cannot be combined with a discount code or with Harbour Plus free delivery. You
get whichever single benefit is worth more.
