# What the stock statuses mean

A product page shows one of these:

- **In stock** — in the warehouse now. Ordered before 1 p.m. on a working day, it is dispatched
  the same day.
- **Low stock** — fewer than ten units. Still dispatched the same day, but the page may change
  while you are on it.
- **Available to order** — not in the warehouse; we order it from the supplier when you do.
  Dispatch is quoted on the page and is usually 7 to 14 days.
- **Back in stock soon** — out of stock with a delivery booked from the supplier. You can ask to
  be emailed and cannot order yet.
- **Discontinued** — we are not selling it again. Any stock showing is the last of it.

A status is not a promise. An item can sell out between your adding it to a basket and paying,
and if that happens we cancel that line and refund it rather than hold your order.
