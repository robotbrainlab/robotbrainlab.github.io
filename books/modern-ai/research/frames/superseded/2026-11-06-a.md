## Selection rule (not an importance ordering)

Every category named by any of S1–S7 was pooled. A category is reported here only if at
least four of the seven source-sets carry it under a name that is plainly the same
category. That threshold yields 10 areas, under the cap of 12. Categories attested by
three or fewer source-sets are listed at the end, by name and count, not reported as
units. The threshold is cross-source attestation only; it says nothing about activity,
size or merit.
