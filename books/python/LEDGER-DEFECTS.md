# Ledger — what the gates cannot catch

One entry per class of defect found in this book, with how it was found and what now
prevents it. **Read before writing a chapter.**

**This file starts empty, and that is deliberate.** The finished book's
`../modern-ai-engineering-from-zero/docs/LEDGER-DEFECTS.md` holds **99** entries, earned on
that book. Ten of those were themselves inherited from an earlier book; **89 were earned while its 57 chapters were written**. They are **read as evidence, never inherited as this book's record**: an entry
here means it happened here. Copying them would make this file a summary, and a summary of
a defect is the thing that stops anybody looking.

**Three entries from that book are worth reading before chapter 1**, because they are
about the method rather than about its subject:

- **Entry 18** — a gate that cries wolf is a gate nobody reads. It is why the word lists
  in `tools/` are hand-written and short.
- **Entry 5a** — an unnamed cut is the worst failure this project has recorded. It is why
  `TOC.md` names all six roadmap projects this book does not set.
- **Entry 35** — never read a gate's verdict from the tail of its output.
